# AI Workforce Roles

One human CEO supervises Executive AI and five engines: Growth, Revenue, Production, Relationship, Control. These engines coordinate **16 logical departments**, not separate hosted model instances.

## Business departments
1. Social Media: content research, calendar, graphics/video, post and analytics.
2. Marketing: campaign research, SEO, marketing analysis.
3. Prospecting: compliant sourcing and qualification of leads.
4. Outbound Messaging: personalized drafts, approval-gated sends.
5. Sales: qualification, proposal drafts, follow-up.
6. Customer Service: approved FAQ, tickets and onboarding.
7. Finance: draft invoices, verified payment records, reports.
8. HR & Resources: capacity and freelancer plans.
9. Project Management: project milestones and handoffs.
10. QA & IT: quality, testing, monitoring and incident reports.

## Delivery departments
11. Web Development: code, tests and PRs; no self-deployment.
12. Mobile Development: Expo/React Native builds and tests.
13. AI & Automation Delivery: n8n and AI workflow delivery.
14. Video & Creative Studio: Remotion, FFmpeg, approved Canva assets.
15. Digital Marketing Delivery: client campaigns and content, spend gated.
16. Maintenance & Support: monitoring, incident response, proposed repairs.

## First team contract
Social Manager prioritizes; Researcher returns timestamped URLs; Content Agent returns structured scripts, captions and CTA; Graphic Agent returns valid media and manifest; Video Agent renders short MP4 and subtitles; QA Agent passes/rejects with evidence; Publishing Agent requires signed-off policy and verified permissions before posting, then records post ID and analytics.

Handoffs include job ID, owner, inputs, outputs, source evidence, warnings, status and next assignee. No role grants its own authority. Every external action is auditable.
