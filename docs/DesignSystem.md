# Design System

Focus first on high-quality Bigyard Digital branded social content, not a large admin dashboard. Brand source: https://bigyarddigital.com/ and Bigyard Digital — Brand & Services in Notion.

Tagline: AI-Powered Digital Growth Studio. Services: Build / Create / Grow. Use confident typography, strong hierarchy, high contrast, clear mobile-readable caption text, one specific CTA, subtle motion.

Media sizes: feed 1080×1350; square 1080×1080; vertical video 1080×1920 at 30 fps, typically 15–30 seconds. Keep subtitles and CTA inside safe margins. Render transparent PNG graphics, animation, captions and optional audio as required. Inspect on mobile.

**Brand hex colors, typography and logo are NOT yet verified.** Get them from official site assets or owner-approved files before claiming brand-perfect output. Only use owned or properly licensed footage, photos, music, fonts and Canva exports. Canva can provide manual visuals and templates but may not provide unattended access.

Video narrative: 0–3 seconds hook; useful explanation and proof; final CTA. QA for clarity, crop, sound-off legibility, aspect ratio, audio quality and factual truth.
